What Is Medicare Advantage?

A Smarter Way to Get Medicare — If It Fits Your Life

Medicare Advantage, also called Part C, is an alternative to Original Medicare offered by private insurance carriers. Instead of Parts A and B paying separately, a Medicare Advantage plan bundles everything into one monthly premium — often as low as $0 — and typically adds benefits Original Medicare does not cover at all.

For many Midlands residents, especially those who are relatively healthy, use in-network providers in the Lexington and Richland county area, and want to keep their monthly costs predictable, Medicare Advantage is an excellent fit. For others — particularly those who see specialists frequently or travel regularly — a Medicare Supplement plan may serve them better.

That is the conversation we have with every Midlands client before we ever recommend a plan. We do not earn more from one plan than another — our job is to find yours.

What Medicare Advantage Plans Typically Include

  • Part A & B coverage — hospital and medical, same as Original Medicare
  • Part D drug coverage — most plans bundle prescription coverage at no extra premium
  • Dental benefits — cleanings, x-rays, and often major dental work
  • Vision coverage — annual eye exams and eyewear allowances
  • Hearing aids & exams — often included at reduced or no cost
  • Fitness memberships — Silver Sneakers or similar programs
  • Transportation benefits — rides to medical appointments
  • Over-the-counter allowances — quarterly credits for health items
  • Telehealth access — virtual visits with physicians

Benefits vary by carrier and plan. We verify what is available in your specific Midlands zip code.

What to Know Before You Choose a Medicare Advantage Plan in Lexington & Richland Counties

Medicare Advantage plans are not one-size-fits-all — and the options available in Lexington County may differ from those available in Richland County, even for communities just a few miles apart. Here is what matters most for Midlands residents.

🏥

Provider Networks

The most important question is whether your doctors — your primary care physician, your cardiologist, your orthopedist — are in-network. We check the network before we ever recommend a plan. Prisma Health, Lexington Medical Center, and MUSC Health all participate in multiple local Medicare Advantage networks.

💊

Your Medications

Every Medicare Advantage plan with drug coverage has a formulary — a list of covered drugs and their cost tiers. We run your specific medication list through every available plan to find the one that keeps your out-of-pocket drug costs lowest. This step alone often saves Midlands clients hundreds of dollars per year.

📍

Your Zip Code Matters

Plan availability and pricing vary by zip code throughout the Midlands. A plan available in Lexington 29072 may not be available in Chapin 29036 or Blythewood 29016. We search your exact zip code — not just the county — to make sure you see every option you actually qualify for.
