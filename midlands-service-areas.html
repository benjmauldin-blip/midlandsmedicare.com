Richland County

Richland County — The Capital City and Its Communities

Richland County is home to Columbia, the state capital, and some of the Midlands' fastest-growing suburban communities. The Richland County Medicare market is one of the most active in South Carolina, with multiple carriers and a wide range of plan options available across the county's diverse zip codes.

Columbia

Zip codes 29201–29229. The state capital. Extensive Medicare plan options across Richland County with access to Prisma Health, MUSC Health Columbia, and more.

Irmo

Zip codes 29063, 29212. One of the Midlands' most popular retirement communities. Access to both Lexington and Richland county provider networks.

Blythewood

Zip codes 29016, 29078. Fast-growing northern Richland County community. Rapidly increasing Medicare-age population.

Northeast Columbia

Zip codes 29223, 29229. Includes communities like Lake Carolina, The Summit, and Denny Terrace. Strong Medicare Advantage competition in this area.

Forest Acres

Zip code 29206. Established Columbia neighborhood adjacent to Fort Jackson. Large population of military retirees with Medicare needs.

Dentsville & Hopkins

Zip codes 29204, 29061. Eastern Richland County communities. We serve residents throughout this area by phone, video, and in-person.

Ballentine & Dutch Fork

Zip code 29002. The Dutch Fork area bridging Lexington and Richland counties. Access to both county plan networks.

Winnsboro (Fairfield Co.)

Zip code 29180. We also serve neighboring Fairfield County communities for clients who prefer working with a Midlands-based local agent.
