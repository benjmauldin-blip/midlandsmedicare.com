Why This Matters

Your Medicare Coverage Depends on Which Type of Plan You Have

Moving to South Carolina isn't just a change of address. For Medicare enrollees, it can mean your entire coverage structure needs to be reviewed — immediately.

🚨 Urgent Action
Medicare Advantage
Medicare Advantage plans are regional. Your out-of-state plan almost certainly has no in-network providers in Lexington County, SC. The only coverage it provides here is emergency care.
→ You have 60 days to switch to an SC plan
⚠️ Verify First
Employer Retiree Plans
Some employer group Medicare Advantage plans say "nationwide coverage" — but that doesn't mean Lexington Medical Center or your new SC doctors are in-network. These plans don't appear on Medicare's public finder.
→ Bring your plan documents and we'll verify
✅ You're in Good Shape
Medicare Supplement (Medigap)
Medigap plans have no provider networks. They follow Original Medicare anywhere — including LMC, Prisma Health, and every Medicare-accepting provider in the SC Midlands. No network to check.
→ Just verify your carrier services SC
