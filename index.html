Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with an all-in-one private plan that typically includes drug coverage and extra benefits like dental and vision, often with low or $0 monthly premiums — but it uses provider networks. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) works alongside Original Medicare, covering the 20% coinsurance and other gaps. It has a monthly premium but lets you see any Medicare-accepting doctor nationwide with very predictable costs. The right choice depends on your health, your doctors, and your budget — we help you decide for free.

Yes — Medicare Annual Enrollment Period runs October 15 through December 7 every year. During this window you can switch Medicare Advantage plans, return to Original Medicare, or change your Part D drug plan. Medicare Part D covers prescription drugs. If you are on Original Medicare, you should enroll in a standalone Part D plan when you first become eligible to avoid a permanent late enrollment penalty — even if you take few medications now. If you choose a Medicare Advantage plan, drug coverage is usually included. We compare Part D formularies across all carriers in the Midlands to find the plan that best matches your specific medications and preferred pharmacies.
