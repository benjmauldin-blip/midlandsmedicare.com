ACA in the Midlands

Affordable Health Insurance for Midlands Residents Under 65

The Affordable Care Act marketplace offers health insurance plans for individuals and families who do not have employer coverage and are not yet eligible for Medicare. For many Midlands residents — including early retirees, self-employed individuals, gig workers, and those between jobs — the ACA marketplace is the primary path to affordable health coverage.

What surprises many Midlands clients is how much financial help is available. Premium tax credits are based on your household income, and in many cases can reduce your monthly premium to $0 or close to it. Enhanced subsidies through recent legislation have made ACA coverage more accessible than ever for South Carolina residents.

South Carolina has historically had some of the highest rates of uninsured residents in the nation. Many Midlands families who think they cannot afford health insurance actually qualify for significant subsidies. We check eligibility for every client at no charge.

Who Should Consider ACA Coverage in the Midlands?

  • Early retirees under 65 who are no longer on employer coverage
  • Self-employed residents of Lexington and Richland counties
  • Part-time workers without employer-sponsored benefits
  • Individuals who lost employer coverage due to a job change
  • Spouses or dependents who lose coverage when a family member retires
  • Bridge coverage for those 60–64 awaiting Medicare eligibility

ACA Open Enrollment & Special Periods

  • Open Enrollment: November 1 – January 15 for the following year
  • Special Enrollment: Within 60 days of a qualifying life event (job loss, marriage, birth, moving to the Midlands)
  • Medicaid/CHIP: May be available year-round for qualifying Midlands residents
Understanding Subsidies

How Much Help Can Midlands Residents Get?

💰

Premium Tax Credits

Available to households with income between 100% and 400% of the federal poverty level (and in many cases above). Credits are applied monthly to reduce your premium. A family of two earning $60,000/year may qualify for hundreds of dollars per month in credits.

🏥

Cost-Sharing Reductions

Available on Silver-tier plans for households below 250% of the federal poverty level. Reduces deductibles, copays, and out-of-pocket maximums — making Silver plans act more like Gold or Platinum plans at Silver prices.

🌿

Medicaid Expansion

South Carolina has not expanded Medicaid, which creates a coverage gap for some low-income Midlands residents. We help clients understand their options and identify the best path to coverage given South Carolina's current Medicaid structure.
