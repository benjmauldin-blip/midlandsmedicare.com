Understanding Medigap

Why Midlands Seniors Choose Medicare Supplement

Original Medicare covers roughly 80% of your healthcare costs. The remaining 20% — plus hospital deductibles, copays, and skilled nursing facility costs — comes out of your pocket unless you have supplemental coverage. For many Midlands seniors, that 20% adds up to thousands of dollars in unpredictable bills each year.

A Medicare Supplement plan, also called Medigap, fills those gaps. You pay a fixed monthly premium, and the plan handles most or all of the cost-sharing Original Medicare leaves behind. For residents of Lexington, Richland, and surrounding counties who see their doctors frequently, have ongoing health conditions, or simply want to know exactly what they will pay, Medigap provides the predictability that Medicare Advantage cannot always guarantee.

And unlike Medicare Advantage, a Medigap policy lets you see any doctor or hospital that accepts Medicare — anywhere in the United States — with no referrals and no network restrictions.

Plan G vs Plan N

The Two Plans Most Midlands Seniors Should Compare

For new Medicare enrollees in the SC Midlands, the choice typically comes down to Plan G and Plan N. Both are excellent plans — the right one depends on how often you use medical care and how much premium savings you want versus out-of-pocket certainty.

Plan G — The Most Comprehensive Option

Plan G covers everything Original Medicare leaves behind except the Part B deductible ($257 in 2026). After you meet that one annual deductible, you have virtually no other out-of-pocket costs for Medicare-covered services.

  • Part A deductible — Covered
  • Part B deductible ($257/yr) — Not covered
  • Part B coinsurance (20%) — Covered
  • Skilled nursing facility coinsurance — Covered
  • Part A hospice coinsurance — Covered
  • Foreign travel emergencies — Covered (80%)
  • Excess charges — Covered

Best for: Midlands residents who see doctors frequently or have ongoing health conditions and want maximum coverage with minimal billing surprises.

Plan N — Lower Premium, Still Strong Coverage

Plan N covers most of what Plan G covers, with two differences: small copays of up to $20 for doctor visits and up to $50 for emergency room visits (waived if admitted). In exchange, Plan N typically carries a notably lower monthly premium.

  • Part A deductible — Covered
  • Part B deductible ($257/yr) — Not covered
  • Part B coinsurance — Covered (with $20 copay)
  • Skilled nursing facility coinsurance — Covered
  • Part A hospice coinsurance — Covered
  • Foreign travel emergencies — Covered (80%)
  • Excess charges — Not covered

Best for: Midlands residents who are relatively healthy, do not mind small predictable copays, and want to keep their monthly premium lower while maintaining strong coverage.

Why the Same Plan Costs Different Amounts from Different Carriers

Plan G is standardized by the federal government — the benefits are identical no matter which carrier you choose. What varies is the premium. Carrier A might charge a 65-year-old Lexington resident $145/month for Plan G while Carrier B charges $189/month for the exact same coverage. Enrollment Timing

When to Enroll in Medigap — This Window Matters

The best time to buy a Medicare Supplement plan is during your Medigap Open Enrollment Period — the 6-month window that begins the month you turn 65 and are enrolled in Medicare Part B. This window is critical for Midlands residents to understand.

✅

During Open Enrollment

Insurance companies cannot deny you coverage or charge you more based on health conditions. You get the best available rate regardless of your medical history. This is the ideal time to enroll.

⚠️

After Open Enrollment

In South Carolina, carriers may use medical underwriting — meaning they can charge you more or deny coverage based on health conditions. Pre-existing conditions can significantly increase your premium or result in declined coverage.

🔄

Guaranteed Issue Rights

Certain life events — losing employer coverage, moving to the Midlands from another state, or your Medicare Advantage plan leaving your area — can trigger guaranteed issue rights that allow you to enroll without underwriting.
