What Is Final Expense Insurance?

Protecting Your Midlands Family From End-of-Life Costs

The average funeral in South Carolina costs between $9,000 and $12,000 — and that is before headstones, obituary notices, cemetery fees, or outstanding medical bills. For most Midlands families, that money does not exist in a savings account labeled "for my funeral." Without a plan, that burden lands on your children, your spouse, or your closest relatives at the worst possible moment.

Final expense insurance — sometimes called burial insurance — is a type of whole life insurance specifically designed to cover those end-of-life costs. It is simple, affordable, and requires no medical exam for most applicants. Coverage typically ranges from $5,000 to $25,000 and premiums start as low as $20–$70 per month depending on your age.

One of the most meaningful things we do at Midlands Medicare is help Lexington, Columbia, and Midlands seniors protect the people they love from financial panic at the moment of grief. Final expense insurance is practical love.

What Final Expense Benefits Can Cover

  • Funeral service, viewing, and burial or cremation
  • Headstone, grave marker, or mausoleum fees
  • Cemetery plot and opening/closing fees
  • Outstanding medical bills from a final illness
  • Outstanding credit card balances or personal debts
  • Memorial service, flowers, and reception costs
  • Travel expenses for out-of-town family members
  • Remaining mortgage or rent payments for a surviving spouse
  • Any remaining funds become a meaningful gift to your beneficiary

Who Qualifies?

  • Midlands residents typically ages 50 to 85
  • No medical exam required for most plans
  • Simplified issue: a few health questions
  • Guaranteed issue: no health questions for those with serious conditions
  • Coverage approved quickly — often within days
